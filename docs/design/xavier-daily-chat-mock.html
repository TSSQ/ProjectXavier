ProjectXavier · design proposal · Assistant · drawn at iPhone 17 Pro Max

Today's chat with Xavier

Every phone here is drawn 1:1 in iOS points at iPhone 17 Pro Max size (440 × 956), with type from the app's own scale at that width: body 19, rows 17, captions 16, prompts 25. Section 3 shrinks the same screen to iPhone 17 and SE. The idea: today's conversation stays as a chat, Xavier's speech bubbles on the left and yours on the right, and it clears itself on the first open of a new day. Your transactions are never affected.

3 · Shrinking from the Pro Max

The same screen on iPhone 17 and iPhone SE

Nothing is redesigned per phone. Sizes come from the app's existing scale (src/domain/scaleMath.ts): type scales with width (×1.12 on Pro Max, ×1.03 on iPhone 17, ×0.96 on SE), and the avatar, padding and composer step down by tier. The SE is the tight case: its 667pt height shows about two exchanges above the live card.

4 · Rules

How the day works

When it resets

On the first open after local midnight, never mid-conversation. Chatting at 23:55 and still going at 00:10 stays one conversation until you leave the app. A draft you never confirmed is dropped at reset, the same rule as moving past it.

What's stored, and where

The day's messages live in the encrypted local database and are deleted at reset. They're left out of iCloud backups, because they're a view of today, not data. Transactions, budgets and accounts are never touched.

Live vs history

Only the newest card or chip row is tappable; everything above is read-only. A card made stale by a data change collapses to its stub instead of vanishing.

Unchanged

Parsing, routing, every card's content and buttons, the composer, the + menu, the camera, the tab bar and Xavier's faces. The approved speech bubble is the message style. Charts from questions stay as cards in the history.

Risk: one screen rebuilt

Today each card type has its own slot in a 4,800-line screen. A feed means one list of messages that each render their own kind. That's about the size of the budgets feature, and needs careful QA of the keyboard, scroll-to-newest and the statement-scan queue.

Taste call: the settle

Today a receipt fades back to the greeting after 5 seconds. In a chat it stays in the history, and only Xavier's face settles. I'd leave out a manual "clear today" control in v1.
