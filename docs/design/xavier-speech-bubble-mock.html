ProjectXavier · design proposal · Assistant

Xavier says it in a speech bubble

Today a saved expense shows a receipt card down by the composer, away from Xavier, while he says "Saved! Anything else?" in plain text above it. This proposal moves the confirmation into one speech bubble from Xavier, budget left included. Every other confirmation (created, updated, archived, budget set, cancelled) uses the same bubble.

Chosen B · every line is a bubble
9:41●●● ▮
Hi, I'm Xavier. Tell me about an expense, snap a receipt or a statement, or tap + for more.

Xavier always speaks the same way. The greeting, questions ("Which budget would this come from?"), errors and confirmations all sit in the bubble, so the bubble is Xavier's voice. One component replaces the reply text, so it's also the simpler build. Confirmations still stand out through their content: the amount, the meter and his happy face.

Unchanged

Xavier's size, faces and position; the 5-second settle and settle-on-typing rules; the composer and tab bar; all decision cards and their buttons. The saved receipt card goes away, because its content moves into the bubble.

Accessibility

The bubble is still text, so VoiceOver reads it as Xavier's line, and Dynamic Type wraps it rather than clipping. It keeps today's "touches pass through" behaviour, so tapping it still dismisses the keyboard like the backdrop does.

Risk: long text at large type sizes

A bubble with a meter at the largest Dynamic Type sizes, plus a decision card below, can push the card under the keyboard. The bubble caps at about 4 lines of body text and the meter line wraps; I'd verify this on device at the largest accessibility size.
