ProjectXavier · design audit

The system exists. It isn’t used.

A full UI consistency audit across 20 screens and 46 components. Every finding here is a good design system being bypassed — not a bad one being followed.

23 Aug 2026 reviewed at 5b7b47b live: v1.1.1 (build 74) 16,085 lines of TSX 22 findings
“Some choices are inconsistent — some are buttons and some are just strong texts.”

That observation is correct, and it is the visible tip of something more specific than sloppiness. The app has 33 semantic colour tokens, a 4-step radius scale, and an 8-role type ramp whose maths is pure, framework-free, and covered by the plain-Node suite — so a transposed base is caught by tests rather than a screenshot. That is better infrastructure than most apps this size have.

Here is how much of it is actually reached:

why

Two clusters aren’t carelessness

The type ramp’s smallest role is caption: 14. The app’s uppercase micro-labels render at 9, 10 and 12px, and its badges at 8, 9, 10 and 11px. There is no role for either.

So every author who needed an overline or a badge invented a size, and 60+ of them did, independently. The ramp is missing its bottom two rungs — which makes adding label and badge roles a precondition for fixing findings 11 and 13, not a follow-up to them. Migrate the call sites first and they drift straight back.

Findings

01

28 tappable targets below 44pt; zero hitSlopP0

Sizes 32–40pt across 13 files. The cheapest fix in this document — hitSlop costs nothing and changes no layout.

02

59 of 66 files ignore Dynamic TypeP0

A user at large accessibility text sizes gets a partially scaled interface — arguably worse than none, because the mismatch is jarring. Three hand-rolled buttons pin a fixed height around a label that does scale, so the label clips inside its own button.

03

144 of 155 Pressables give no touch feedbackP0

Press styling exists in 5 files. Elsewhere a tap produces no acknowledgement until the action completes — and several actions are slow enough for that gap to read as “the tap didn’t register”.

04

rounded-pill means six different thingsP1

Button, badge, tab, filter chip, text link, page indicator. Shape no longer signals tappability. Shown above.

05

Four implementations of “the primary button”, four heightsP1

44 / 40 / 48 / 50pt. Three dropped the 44pt floor, Dynamic Type, or press feedback. Shown above.

06

“Done” rendered four ways across five sheetsP1

Same word, same job, three visual treatments, two colours. Shown above.

07

“Go somewhere” has three affordancesP1

dashboard:548 “Manage” is 12px green text; index:3471 “Show all N” is scaled blue bold text; Settings uses a full row with a chevron.

08

Three confirmation idiomsP1

23 native Alert.alert calls across 12 files (11 destructive), plus the assistant’s in-app confirm card, plus the hand-rolled stacked account-delete surface. The native alert is the right default; the question is why two others exist.

09

The interactive green and the money-in green are the same colourP1

accent #0E8A4F vs positive #149158. In a finance app, a green “Apply” is a genuine misread risk. Shown above.

10

Two tokens both mean “tappable”P1

text-primary (blue, 19 uses) and text-accent (green, 4) used interchangeably. Raw hex is not a problem — 27 literals, 12 of them #fff. Colour discipline is good; the semantics collide.

11

20 distinct text sizes, 12 of them arbitraryP2

Shown above. Most small values exist because the ramp has no role below 14.

12

Three font weights doing two jobsP2

font-bold (93), font-semibold (64), font-extrabold (39). No rule distinguishes them. font-medium appears once, almost certainly by accident.

13

<SectionLabel> bypassed in nine variantsP2

15 uses of the component against 22 hand-rolled, spanning three sizes and six spacing combinations. Shown above.

14

Sheets have three corner radiiP2

rounded-t-3xl (×6), rounded-t-2xl (×2), rounded-t-[22px] (×1, DateField). Sheets are the most-seen surface; the difference is visible when two open in sequence.

15

Combobox uses the wrong groundP2

bg-bg where all eight other sheets use bg-surface — one sheet sits a shade darker than the rest.

16

Radius scale is 4 tokens; 6 more values in useP2

Tokens: sm/md/lg/pill. Also present: rounded-full (34), bare rounded (18), rounded-xl (13), rounded-2xl (3), plus two arbitrary. rounded-full and rounded-pill are the same shape reached two ways.

17

Four expressions of “disabled”P2

opacity 0.35, 0.55, 0.6, and a colour swap to bg-surfaceAlt/text-muted. Only 10 controls set disabled at all.

18

Empty states: consistent voice, inconsistent everything elseP3

All 14 read well — the copy is genuinely good. But they span four sizes, two alignments and five padding values, and only one of 14 offers an action. The rest are dead ends.

19

Loading labels vary in formP3

'Saving…', 'Deleting…', 'Preparing…' vs lowercase 'loading…'. 16 ActivityIndicators with no shared wrapper.

20

13 icon sizes for roughly three jobsP3

16 (×33), 18 (×21), 14 (×15), 15 (×9), 24, 20, 13, 22… The 15/16 and 13/14 pairs are indistinguishable on screen.

21

Byte-identical triplicationP3

flex-row items-center bg-surfaceAlt border border-border rounded-pill px-3.5 py-2 appears verbatim in dashboard:321, transactions:565 and debug-metrics:62.

22

Three dead componentsP3

ListRow, Bubble, FeedRecord — zero importers. ListRow in particular is the row primitive that would have prevented several findings above.

.eslintrc.js already proves a lint rule can catch a whole bug class here — the NativeWind function-form-style ban exists precisely because that failure was invisible to every other gate. The same lever applies: ban arbitrary text-[Npx] in favour of ramp roles, and flag rounded-pill + onPress outside Button.

order

Suggested order

  1. hitSlop on the 28 undersized targets. Hours, no layout change. Closes the only P0 affecting every user with imprecise touch.
  2. Retire accent as an interactive colour. Four call sites. Removes a green-means-two-things collision in a finance app.
  3. Add label + badge roles to the ramp, then migrate the 60+ hardcoded small sizes.
  4. Fold the three hand-rolled buttons into <Button>. Recovers the 44pt floor and Dynamic Type at each site.
  5. Split the pill into distinct Badge / Chip / Button primitives. The largest change, and the one that actually answers the original question.
  6. The rest. Press feedback; sheet radius; empty states; delete or wire the dead components.

Steps 1–2 are a day and fix real defects. Steps 3–5 are the structural work, and they are what “consistency” actually costs.
